UTM JOHOR BAHRU — ON-CAMPUS SHUTTLE BUS SERVICES
Source: Unit UTM Fleet, Jabatan TNC(P). Effective schedule from 6 October 2025.

GENERAL RULES (apply to every route below unless stated otherwise):
- No bus service on Saturday, Sunday, public holidays, or during semester breaks
  (except routes BAS E1 and E2, which have a separate, reduced Saturday & Sunday
  schedule — see the end of this document).
- On Fridays, no trips run between 12:40 PM and 2:00 PM on any route.
- Arrival times may vary due to traffic.

Location code reference (confirmed against Useful-Contact.pdf where possible):
- CP = Center Point
- KP = Kolej Perdana
- K9/10 = Kolej 9 & 10
- KTC = Kolej Tuanku Canselor
- KTHO = Kolej Tun Hussein Onn
- KTDI = Kolej Tun Dr. Ismail
- KTR = Kolej Tun Razak
- KDOJ = likely Kolej Dato' Onn Jaafar (initials match; not explicitly confirmed in source)
- KDSE = likely Kolej Datin Seri Endon (initials match; not explicitly confirmed in source)
- KLG, FKT, FKE, V01 (TMN U), FABU = could not be confidently confirmed from available
  documents — verify locally before relying on these in answers (FKT/FKE are likely
  faculty building codes; TMN U likely "Taman Universiti").
